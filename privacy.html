Privacy Policy

Classic Launcher for Mac

SupportPrivacy

In short

Classic Launcher doesn’t collect, sell or share any information about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn’t send anything to the developer. Everything it keeps stays on your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

To work, Classic Launcher keeps a few things in its own storage on your Mac, where only it can use them:

  • Your layout: the pages, the folders and their names, and the order of your apps.
  • Your settings, such as the grid size, transparency, keyboard shortcut and hot corner.
  • Which apps it has already seen, so newly installed ones can be marked as new.
  • If you allow uninstalling, permission to use your Applications folder, so it can move the apps you choose to the Trash.

Deleting Classic Launcher deletes all of this.

What it looks at, and why

  • Your apps. It reads the apps in your Applications folders, with their names and icons, to show them, and notices apps being installed to show their progress.
  • Your desktop picture. It blurs it to draw behind the grid.
  • The pointer’s position, to notice when it reaches the hot corner you’ve turned on. Without a hot corner it’s ignored, and it’s never recorded.

Classic Launcher doesn’t record your screen, read what you type (its shortcut is a standard system shortcut), or look inside your files.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are in-app purchases handled by Apple through the App Store. Classic Launcher only asks the App Store whether the trial has started or the app has been unlocked. It never sees your Apple Account, your payment details or your name. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Internet connections

Classic Launcher has no servers and contains no third-party code. Its only connection is to Apple’s App Store, for purchases.

When you contact support

Need Help? Contact Us… in the app’s … menu opens a new message in your own mail app or in Gmail, addressed to support. To help answer you, the message already includes the app’s version, your macOS version, your Mac’s model, your screen sizes and the launcher’s settings. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself, and you can change or delete any of it first.

Emails you send are used only to answer you and are never shared.

Children

Classic Launcher collects no information from anyone, including children.
